Premium 12-question Love Language Test that reveals your primary love language with a full 5-language breakdown and how-to-speak-it advice.

== Description ==

Drop the shortcode `[love_language_test]` on any page or post. Aliases: `[love_language_quiz]`, `[love_language]`.

Features:
* Engaging one-question-at-a-time quiz (12 forced-choice questions) with a progress bar and Back button
* Reveals your primary love language: Words of Affirmation, Quality Time, Receiving Gifts, Acts of Service, Physical Touch
* Full percentage breakdown across all 5 languages with animated, color-coded bars
* "How You Feel Loved" + "How To Love You Back" panels
* Actionable tips to make your love language work
* Confetti, WhatsApp/Twitter/Save/Copy share, related tools, live counter
* Mobile-first, no jQuery, 100% client-side
* JavaScript delivered via wp_enqueue_script (footer) so wpautop never breaks it
* No FAQ block (so it never duplicates your own SEO content)

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `love-language-test` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/` or upload the ZIP via Plugins -> Add New.
2. Activate the plugin in WordPress.
3. Add `[love_language_test]` to any page, post, or widget.
